01 Genesis

The Lost Vernacular

During the latter half of the twentieth century, rapid urban speculation swept through ancient trade cities. The venerable courtyard house—an aerodynamic sanctuary constructed of dense limestone that shielded occupants from arid desert winds—was discarded in favor of imported steel frames and mirrored glass.

These generic monoliths demanded continuous mechanical refrigeration and severed families from the restorative rhythm of changing skies. Diyari was born as an active resistance: an unyielding commitment to identify, safeguard, and resurrect structures that honor natural environmental equilibrium.

03 Methodology

Curation as Stewardship

Conventional brokerages measure inventory by volume and gross commissions. Diyari functions as an archival institution and advisory council. Less than four percent of reviewed properties are admitted into our collection.

Our architectural conservationists conduct thorough multi-layer provenance investigations: scrutinizing original title ledgers, structural load capacities, thermal envelope dynamics, and historical craftsmanship before an estate is offered to our private circle of collectors.
